Grational Community Rules

These rules explain what Grational users may post and how we expect people to treat each other. They are community rules, not legal advice or a guarantee that the service complies with every law.

Important: This page does not verify anyone’s age or automatically moderate content. A rules page by itself cannot enforce age restrictions or guarantee legal compliance.

1. Age policy

Grational’s general minimum age is 13, where local law permits. Users in Malaysia or Indonesia must be at least 16 to use Grational, subject to applicable local requirements. Do not provide a false age or use the service if you are below the applicable minimum.

This is Grational’s stated policy. The website does not currently check a user’s age or location.

2. Video rating labels

Creators should choose an honest viewer-guidance label for each video. These labels use Malaysian film-classification names as general guidance only. A creator-selected label is not an official LPF classification or approval.

U — General audiences

Intended to be suitable for viewers of all ages.

P12 — Parental guidance

Parental guidance may be appropriate for younger viewers.

13 — Viewers aged 13 and above

Intended for viewers aged 13 or older.

16 — Viewers aged 16 and above

Intended for viewers aged 16 or older.

18 — Adults aged 18 and above

Intended for adults. A rating does not make prohibited content acceptable.

If you are unsure which label fits, choose the more restrictive one. Labels are guidance, not age-gating.

3. Content that is not allowed

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, or sexual content involving minors.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, credible threats, harassment, stalking, or doxxing.
  • Scams, phishing, malware, impersonation, or attempts to steal money or personal information.
  • Content promoting terrorism, violence, self-harm, criminal activity, or dangerous drugs.
  • Content that violates another person’s privacy, copyright, or other rights.
  • Spam, artificial engagement, or attempts to bypass account or safety restrictions.

4. Reporting and enforcement

If you see content that may violate these rules, contact Grational through Discord and include the page link and a description of the concern. Do not download or redistribute suspected illegal material.

Grational may remove content or restrict accounts that violate these rules, subject to applicable law. Do not use Grational to seek emergency help; contact local emergency services.

5. Privacy

Public profiles may show a creator’s chosen username, bio, social links, and published videos. Do not publish passwords, authentication codes, private keys, or another person’s private information.

6. Featured creators

Grational plans to randomly select 20 creators who opt in each month for a featured sponsorship. Selection is not guaranteed. This feature may not be active until it has been implemented.

7. Changes to these rules

Grational may update these rules. Please check this page for the current version.
